# Privacy Policy

## 1. Introduction

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how CV Builder ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you use our service.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

-   Name and contact details
-   Professional experience and qualifications
-   Account credentials
-   Communication preferences

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information

Our service automatically collects certain information, including:

-   Device information (browser type, operating system)
-   Log data (access times, pages viewed)
-   Authentication tokens for maintaining your login session

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

-   Create and manage your account
-   Generate your CV using our AI technology
-   Provide customer support
-   Improve our services
-   Ensure platform security
-   Communicate important updates

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Storage Duration

-   We retain your information only for as long as necessary to provide our services
-   Account information is kept until you request deletion
-   Backup data may be retained for up to 30 days after deletion

### 4.2 Security Measures

We protect your data using:

-   Industry-standard encryption
-   Secure server infrastructure
-   Regular security audits
-   Access control mechanisms

## 5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

### 5.1 We Do Not Share Your Data

We do not share your personal information with third parties except:

-   When required by law
-   To protect our rights or property
-   With your explicit consent

### 5.2 External Links

-   Our website may contain links to external sites
-   We are not responsible for third-party privacy practices
-   We encourage you to review privacy policies of external sites

## 6. Account Deletion and Data Retention

### 6.1 Account Deletion Process

To delete your account and personal information:

1. Email your request to support@cvbuilder.co.za
2. Include your account email in the request
3. We will confirm deletion within 5 business days

### 6.2 Data Retention After Deletion

-   Personal information is permanently deleted
-   Anonymous usage data may be retained
-   Backup data is automatically purged within 30 days

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

-   Access your personal information
-   Request corrections to your data
-   Request deletion of your data
-   Withdraw consent for data processing
-   Receive a copy of your data
-   Lodge a complaint with relevant authorities

## 8. Changes to This Policy

### 8.1 Policy Updates

-   We may update this policy periodically
-   Changes will be effective upon posting
-   Significant changes will be notified via email
-   Last updated date will be modified accordingly

### 8.2 Notification of Changes

-   Material changes will be announced on our website
-   Continued use after changes implies acceptance
-   Previous versions will be archived

## 9. Contact Information

For privacy-related inquiries:

-   Email: support@cvbuilder.co.za
-   Subject line: "Privacy Policy Query"

Response time: Within 48 business hours

## 10. Acceptance of Policy

By using CV Builder, you acknowledge that you have read and understand this Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
