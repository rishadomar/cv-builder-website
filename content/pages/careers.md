# Join Our Mission

At CV Builder, we're pioneering the future of CV creation through AI technology, and we're looking for passionate individuals to join us on this journey.

## Partnership Opportunities

While we don't currently have specific job openings, we're actively seeking partners who share our vision and can bring their expertise to our platform.

### Who We're Looking For

We're particularly interested in collaborating with experts in:

**Human Resources**

-   Recruitment specialists who understand what makes CVs stand out
-   HR professionals with experience in talent acquisition
-   Career counselors and professional development experts

**Design**

-   UI/UX designers passionate about user experience
-   Visual designers who understand professional document aesthetics
-   Template designers with a eye for clean, effective layouts

**Technology**

-   Developers interested in AI and automation
-   Full-stack developers with React/Next.js experience
-   AI enthusiasts who want to shape the future of CV writing

## Why Partner With Us?

-   **Innovation**: Be part of shaping how AI transforms professional document creation
-   **Impact**: Help job seekers present themselves in the best possible light
-   **Growth**: Join a growing platform with exciting development opportunities
-   **Flexibility**: Collaborate in ways that work for you

## How to Express Interest

If you're passionate about any of these areas and interested in partnering with CV Builder, we'd love to hear from you.

Send us an email at support@cvbuilder.co.za with:

-   Your area of expertise
-   How you'd like to contribute
-   Your relevant experience
-   Your ideas for CV Builder
